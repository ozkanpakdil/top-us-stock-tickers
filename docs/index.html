Indicator guide

RSI
Speed and size of recent price moves; overbought (>70) or oversold (<30).
HRSI
Faster RSI on the Hull Moving Average; below 35 = weak momentum, above 65 = strong.
MFI
Price + volume gauge of buying/selling pressure; high = heavy buying, low = selling.
Stoch %K
Latest close vs recent high-low range; signals where momentum is heading.

Oversold / Overbought = extreme levels for RSI, MFI and Stoch; Weak / Strong = momentum strength for HRSI.
